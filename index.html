Common questions

Answers before you book

Still deciding? Here's what most clients ask us first.

Do you offer services in both English and Spanish?

Yes — every consultation, form, and explanation is available in the language you're most comfortable using, from your first call through your final signature.

How much does tax preparation cost?

Flat-fee pricing starts at $149 for a straightforward individual return. We'll give you an exact quote after a free consultation — and new clients save an extra $20 on their first tax preparation.

I already have an IRS notice or owe back taxes — can you help?

Yes. Our IRS Resolution service covers notices, audits, back taxes, and payment plans — we communicate with the IRS directly on your behalf so you don't have to.

Are you immigration attorneys?

No — we are not immigration attorneys. Our team provides document preparation, translation, and guidance to help you understand and complete immigration paperwork accurately. For matters requiring legal representation, we can refer you to licensed immigration attorneys we trust.
